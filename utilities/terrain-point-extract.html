How the obstacles are built

Obstacle centres are laid out on a triangular lattice with spacing √3 × radius, so circles of the chosen radius overlap just enough to leave no gaps. A centre is kept when its circle touches the ring or polygon. The elevation is the maximum of every DEM pixel the circle touches (pixels that merely clip the circle count too), which is conservative for small radii. Points whose elevation is below the minimum, or with no DEM coverage, are dropped, and the remainder are numbered sequentially before being split into files.

Coordinates are computed in the UTM zone of the ARP; output is WGS84 decimal degrees to 6 dp, elevations in metres to 2 dp: IDENTIFIER, DESCRIPTION, LATITUDE, LONGITUDE, ELEVATION, ELEVATIONUNITS, RADIUS, RADIUSUNITS.
