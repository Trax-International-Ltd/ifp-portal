DVOF Duplicate Filter

Strip DVOF obstacles that duplicate a CAP1732 surveyed obstacle, within a horizontal and height tolerance. The CAP1732 survey is never altered. Outputs are PDToolkit-ready .pds files.

All processing runs in your browser. No data leaves this page.

Datasets

Tolerance

A DVOF obstacle recorded much taller than the surveyed one nearby is kept, so a height isn't lost by the filter.
